## Exploratory experiments

This directory contains selected experiments that were conducted during model development but are not discussed in the thesis.

These include:

- Training with PPO from stable-baselines3 and sb3_contrib
- Training with base CleanRL PPO implementation
- Training with earlier custom PPO implementations
- Evaluation of the resulting trained models

These notebooks are included for transparency and potential research interest. They are not required to reproduce the thesis results and may be less polished than the notebooks in the repository's `notebooks/` directory.

A method's inclusion in this directory does not imply that the method is generally ineffective. It only indicates that it did not perform satisfactorily under the tested implementation, environment, hyperparameters, and computational budget.
